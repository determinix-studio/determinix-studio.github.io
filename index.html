The Studio

Systems you can trust to keep going.

Determinix Studio is a small, independent game studio. We make original games for iOS and Android, and we stay with each one long after launch.

Our name is our approach. We're drawn to deterministic worlds: games built on clear, dependable systems that behave the same way every time, keep running while you're away, and reward the curiosity you bring back to them.

Determinix Studio is the games division of Determinix Holdings LLC, a Wyoming company.

  • Systems over spectacleDeep, legible mechanics you can actually master.
  • Made to lastFewer games, supported for the long haul.
  • Fair by defaultClear privacy, honest pricing, no dark patterns.
