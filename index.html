Studio

Original games, crafted in-house

Determinix Studio is an independent game studio. We design, build, and ship original games for iOS and Android — carrying each title from its first prototype through to the updates that follow long after launch.

We're drawn to atmospheric, mechanically rich games with a strong sense of place: worlds that reward curiosity and keep unfolding the more time you spend in them.

Determinix Studio is the games division of Determinix Holdings LLC, a Wyoming company.
