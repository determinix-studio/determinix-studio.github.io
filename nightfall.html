About the Game

Dreams don't sleep. Neither does your machine.

When the sun goes down, the Dream Machine wakes up. In Nightfall: Dream Machine, you take charge of a strange and beautiful contraption that gathers the fading light of dusk and weaves it into dreams.

Start with a single flicker of light and gradually expand your machine — adding gears, collectors, and dream-spinners that keep working long after you put your phone down. Every session you return to a world that has quietly grown while you were away, ready to be reinvested into bigger, stranger, more wonderful dreams.

It's a calm, rewarding loop designed for a few minutes at a time: tune your machine, unlock the next upgrade, and watch your dream world unfold at its own gentle pace.

Features

  • Relaxing idle gameplay that progresses even while you're away
  • Dozens of upgrades and automations to unlock
  • A hand-crafted, atmospheric dusk-to-night aesthetic
  • Meaningful offline progress every time you return
  • No pressure, no timers — play at your own pace
  • Designed for quick sessions on iPhone and Android
