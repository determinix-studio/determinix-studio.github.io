The Story

Every night, you go under.

A box you weren't meant to open

A grandfather you never met left a box behind, and one rule that came down with it: nobody opens it. No one could say why anymore. You opened it anyway — and under the ordinary keepsakes, wrapped like something to be ashamed of, was an object that shouldn't be warm. You didn't decide to keep it. It just ended up an inch from your pillow.

Down past the dream

Asleep beside it, you don't dream at all. You fall past dreaming, into the Undermind — the shared floor beneath every sleeping mind, the place where the waking world and every dream are actually made. Almost no one has ever reached it. Something has been running there all along.

The machine, and the one before you

A Machine works the Undermind alone, drawing up its raw Current and shaping it into the enemies sent to drive you out. You can't touch any of it until you go lucid — awake inside the dream. And once you are, a worn stair tells you someone stood here first: the grandfather you never met, the one who found this place and learned the single thing the Machine never could — how to refine the Current into power of your own.

Whoever holds the Current

Break what the Machine sends and it leaves a residue behind. Catch it in the Refinery you build, and the power is yours; leave it, and the Machine takes it back, stronger. Getting stronger and arming your enemy are the same act — the only choice that matters is who the Current belongs to. It's the work your grandfather gave decades to in secret, and the one thing he tried to keep from ever reaching you.

The world doesn't stay neutral on its own. It leans toward carelessness — because the only mind that ever held it never held it for anyone but itself.

Features

What you'll do

Real-time dream combat

Endless waves of Siphons, auto-fought in a lucid arena, with a greater Siphon every fifth wave.

Three pillars of power

Shape your Dreamer around Attack, Defense, or Harvesting — and grow both the night you're in and every night that comes after.

The Refinery

Catch the Dust of everything you break and refine it into Current — across seven elements, each climbing toward greater power.

Dream Forms

Research the Blueprints the Machine never had, focus your forms into the fight, and keep them alive on a constant flow of Current.

Your progress arms both sides

Every scrap of power you don't claim, the Machine takes — and sends back stronger. Outbuild it, or arm it.

Idle that respects your time

Research and refining advance while you're away, so every return starts stronger.

How It Plays

A single night, three moves

Enter the dream

Drop into the lucid arena and stand against endless waves of Siphons in real time, growing stronger with every kill as the night escalates.

Wake and carry

When the night ends, you wake — and keep a little of what you earned, carried into power that lasts across every night that follows.

Refine and research

Refine what you gathered and research the designs the Machine never learned — work that keeps advancing even while you sleep.

FAQ

Questions

What kind of game is it?

A minimalist idle action game: real-time, wave-based dream combat wrapped in a deep world of refining, research, and the long climb through the night.

When does it come out?

Not yet, but it's close. There's no public date to share just yet. Email support@determinix.com and we'll tell you the moment it's ready.

What platforms?

iPhone and Android, releasing on the App Store and Google Play.

Do I need to be online?

No. Nightfall is single-player with offline progress, so your research and refining keep advancing whether you're connected or not.
