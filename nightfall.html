The Game

Your own progress arms both sides.

A machine in the deep of sleep

Something not of this world breached into your Undermind — the deep part of the mind that sleep opens up. It runs there: a smooth, intact Machine that draws raw Dream Current straight from your sleeping mind and shapes it into enemies — Siphons. It has done this alone, uncontested, for as long as it has been there. You can't touch any of it until you go lucid — and then you can do the one thing the Machine never learned: refine.

Potency without loyalty

Every Siphon you break shatters into Dust — which used to scatter and go to waste. You built the Refinery to catch it and refine it up a five-grade ladder into stronger Current. But refined power keeps no loyalty: claim it and it fuels your Dream Forms; leave it, and the Machine pours that same potency into a deadlier Siphon. Every step you take arms both sides — mastery is knowing when to press.

Walls that teach

Nightfall is a minimalist idle game of clean, exponential math — no content treadmill, no relearning how to play. When more-of-the-same stops working, you hit a wall on purpose, and the wall points at the next thing to master. Breaking through feels like insight, not grind — and something you set in motion keeps working while you sleep.

The Machine can shape Current. It has never learned to refine it. That single gap is the entire reason the Dreamer matters.

— From the world of Nightfall
Features

What you'll do

Real-time dream combat

Endless waves of Siphons, auto-fought in a lucid arena, with a greater Siphon every fifth wave.

Three pillars, twelve dials

Build around Attack, Defense, or Harvesting — and tune both session and permanent power.

The Refinery

Refine Dust into graded Current across seven elements, each climbing its own five-grade ladder.

Dream Forms

Research Blueprints, focus forms into your build, and keep them fueled with continuous Current.

The arms-both-sides contest

Your own refinement raises the enemy's ceiling. Outbuild the Machine, or feed it.

Idle that respects your time

Research and refining advance while you're away, so every return starts stronger.

Elements

Seven elements, five grades each

Water, fire, earth and air flow from the first night. Shadow, arcane and void reveal themselves deeper in the dream.

How It Plays

A single night, three moves

Enter the dream

Drop into the arena and fight endless waves of Siphons in real time. Spend Coins on session power to snowball the run.

Wake and bank

When the run ends, unspent Coins fade into Tokens. Bank them into permanent power for every night that follows.

Refine and research

Turn collected Dust into Current, and spend Stones on Research to raise your ceiling — even while you sleep.

FAQ

Questions

What kind of game is it?

A minimalist idle action game: real-time, wave-based dream combat wrapped in a deep economy of refining, research, and compounding upgrades.

When does it come out?

It's in active development — no public date yet. Email support@determinix.com and we'll let you know when it's ready.

What platforms?

iPhone and Android, releasing on the App Store and Google Play.

Do I need to be online?

No. Nightfall is single-player with offline progress — your research and refining keep advancing whether you're connected or not.
